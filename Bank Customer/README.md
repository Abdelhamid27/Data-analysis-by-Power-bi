# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze customer churn behavior, identify retention opportunities, and provide business insights through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for businesses. This dashboard helps decision-makers understand customer behavior by analyzing demographic information, customer activity, credit score, account balance, and retention patterns.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of customer distribution, churn indicators, and retention performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn behavior.
- Measure customer retention performance.
- Identify key factors affecting churn.
- Monitor customer demographics.
- Explore customer activity and banking behavior.
- Support business decisions using interactive dashboards.
